# GCSE Maths Mastery System — Higher

**Mr Nathan's Maths Tuition** — a complete, self-marking GCSE Maths Higher study tool. Study a worked example, test yourself on it, then prove you've actually learned the method with a freshly randomised test that marks itself instantly.

No account, no login, no installation — just open the page in any browser, on any device.

---

## What's inside

**33 chapters, 340 skills** — the complete GCSE Maths Higher curriculum, in the style of WJEC, Edexcel, AQA and IGCSE, including everything in the Foundation curriculum too (exactly as real GCSE Higher tier does) — plus a run of bridging chapters (calculus, formal proof, further probability) that carry a strong student right up to the edge of A-Level.

Every skill follows the same three-step loop:

1. **Study Example** — a fully worked model answer, explained step by step.
2. **Example Test** — the same numbers, so you can prove to yourself you understood the method.
3. **Topic Test** — freshly randomised numbers, marked instantly. This is the real test of whether you've learned the method, not just memorised one answer.

## How to use it

Just open this page — there's nothing to download or install. Choose a chapter from the left, then work through Study Example → Example Test → Topic Test for each skill.

Your progress is saved automatically in your browser, on the device you're using — it's private and never leaves your computer. If you switch devices or browsers, you'll start building progress again there.

## A note on exam boards

The style and difficulty level of every question is written to match WJEC, Edexcel, AQA and IGCSE GCSE Maths — but these badges describe the general style and level of a question, not certification against one specific, current, dated specification. Always check your own exam board's current specification for definitive guidance.
